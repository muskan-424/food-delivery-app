# Tomato Food Delivery — Help & FAQ

## How do I track my order?

Open **My Orders** in the app or ask the assistant "where is my order". You will see the current status: pending, confirmed, preparing, ready, out for delivery, or delivered.

## How do I pay for my order?

Tomato supports Razorpay checkout, wallet balance, and cash on delivery where enabled. Group orders support split pay so each member can pay their share.

## What if my order is late?

Check the order timeline for ETA updates. If the order is significantly delayed, contact support or open a dispute from the order details page.

## Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel while the order is still pending or confirmed (restaurant policy may apply). Refunds for online payments are processed back to the original payment method when approved.

## Delivery verification

Drivers may confirm delivery with OTP, photo proof, or signature. Funds held in escrow (when enabled) release after delivery verification passes.

## KYC and payouts

Restaurant owners and drivers can complete user KYC and register a bank account for RazorpayX payouts after orders are verified.

## Account security

Enable email OTP verification and use a strong password. You can manage notification preferences per category (orders, payments, promos).
